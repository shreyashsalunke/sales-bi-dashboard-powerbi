# Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive two-page Power BI dashboard for analyzing sales, COGS and profit, with drill-down views by time, product, segment and geography.

## Dashboard Pages

**1. Sales Overview**
- Quarterly Sales, COGS and Profit comparison
- Sales share by product
- Units sold by segment
- Profit by product
- Filters by year, product and segment

**2. Detailed Analysis**
- Pivot table of sales by segment and product
- Map of sales by country and segment
- Drill-down detail table by country, segment and product
- Filters by year and discount band

## Key Features
- Year, quarter and month drill-down using a date hierarchy
- Interactive slicers for year, product, segment and discount band
- DAX measures for total sales and year-over-year growth %
- Geographic analysis using a country, state, region and city hierarchy

## Tools
Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Query, Excel

## Data
The dataset is sample data. The report uses two tables: `financials` (segment, product, discount band, units sold, COGS, profit) and `Orders` (category, sub-category, region, location hierarchy).


## How to Use
1. Download `Sales_BI_REPORT.pbix`.
2. Open it in Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the slicers to filter by year, product, segment or discount band.
